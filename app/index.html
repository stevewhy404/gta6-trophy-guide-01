Sources. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official list as PlayStation publishes them (via Exophase); progress targets and counts come from PSNProfiles. Missable rule. A trophy is flagged missable when it is tied to a single named story mission or a one-off story beat. Story missions can usually be replayed, so treat a flag as “do it when it comes”, not “gone forever”.

Offline. This atlas is static files only: it loads nothing from the network, keeps your ticks in this browser's local storage, and never asks for an account. No Rockstar artwork — the medals are drawn with CSS.
